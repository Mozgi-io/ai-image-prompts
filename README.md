## How to use

1. Open a prompt and copy the text.
2. If it has a **[reference photo]** slot, attach your own portrait — the prompt tells the model to keep the face unchanged.
3. Replace anything in `[square brackets]` with your own details: outfit, place, colors.
4. Keep the aspect ratio from the card (most are 4:5).

Works best in GPT Image 2.5 (ChatGPT) and Nano Banana 2.1 (Gemini). Other image models accept them too; results will differ.
